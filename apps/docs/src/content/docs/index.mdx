OpenSignet is the agent-native identity and communications infrastructure.

Developers can integrate directly through APIs and SDKs, people can guide agents, and autonomous
workflows can operate within configured permissions. Domains, DNS, email, optional onchain names,
and the OpenSignet Pass support that foundation. Registration is quoted and paid in USDC on Base through x402.

People sign in with Google, an email code or a wallet, and manage every identity their account owns
from one place. Plans apply to the whole account. See [core concepts](/concepts/#accounts).

The public packages are prepared at version 0.1.0 and are not yet published to npm or PyPI;
until they are, the guides' source-checkout instructions apply.
